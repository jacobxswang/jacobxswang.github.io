About

I am a faculty member at Taiyuan University of Technology. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen).
My research focuses on AI for wireless communications, including semantic communications, intelligent wireless transmission, and mobile network analysis and optimization. I am particularly interested in how learning-based, generative, and multimodal models can improve communication efficiency, reliability, and security under realistic system constraints. My previous work spans image and video semantic communication, receiver-driven protocols, physical- and link-layer cross-layer design, optical wireless communications, and network coding. Earlier, I studied DNN-based physical-layer network coding (PNC) through both simulation and USRP-based implementation.
